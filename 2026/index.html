Welcome to the 18th Conference on Reversible Computation

July 9–10, 2026, Torino, Italy

The Conference on Reversible Computation will bring together researchers from computer science, mathematics, engineering, and physics to discuss new developments and directions for future research in the emerging area of Reversible Computation. This particularly includes applications of reversibility in quantum computation. Research papers, tool demonstrations, and work-in-progress reports are within the scope of the conference, as detailled in our call for papers.

Past editions of this event took place as workshops in York (2009), Bremen (2010), Ghent (2011), Copenhagen (2012), and as conference in Victoria (2013), Kyoto (2014), Grenoble (2015), Bologna (2016), Kolkata (2017), Leicester (2018), Lausanne (2019), Oslo (2020), Nagoya (2021), Urbino (2022), Giessen (2023), Toruń (2024) and Odense (2025). Their proceedings can easily be accessed through dblp and springer (since 2011).

Proceedings

The Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Reversible Computation (RC 2026), published as volume 16626 of Lecture Notes in Computer Science, are available online at Springer website. Free access is granted from this website until August 15th, 2026, and an “interim version” is available until the proceedings are actually available from Springer.
